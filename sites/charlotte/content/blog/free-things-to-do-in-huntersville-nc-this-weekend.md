There are always lots of things to do in and around Huntersville, NC, whether you’re by yourself, with a special date, or need some activities for the entire family! If you’re on a tight budget, you might be surprised at how many free things there are to do in town and a short drive away, both indoors and out.

Check out this quick list of free or low-cost things to do in and around Huntersville, NC, on the weekend or during the week, so you’re never without something to keep you entertained. Hours and events change, so check each one before you go.

## Party in the Park
::post-photo{src="/images/photos/daingerfield-moonlight-mint-museum.jpg" alt="Moonlight, a painting by Elliott Daingerfield in the Mint Museum collection"}
::

A short drive south in Charlotte, the Mint Museum Randolph has hosted Party in the Park afternoons with music, food trucks and free admission to the museum. It’s an excellent choice for anyone looking for both indoor and outdoor activities! The museum also has free-admission times during the year, so check the Mint Museum’s calendar for what’s coming up.

## Charlotte Liberty Walk
::post-photo{src="/images/photos/charlotte-mecklenburg-declaration-signer-plaque.jpg" alt="Bronze plaque honoring Robert Irwin, a signer of the Mecklenburg Declaration"}
::

The Charlotte Liberty Walk, in uptown Charlotte, is an excellent choice for history buffs, and for anyone who wants to get outside, stretch their legs and enjoy some fresh air! Its markers point out sites from the Revolutionary War years, when the British called Charlotte a “hornet’s nest” of rebellion. To find out more, including where the markers are, visit:

[https://charlottecultureguide.com/event/11854/charlotte-liberty-walk](https://charlottecultureguide.com/event/11854/charlotte-liberty-walk)

## Buzzard Rock Trail
::post-photo{src="/images/photos/charlotte-irwin-creek-greenway.jpg" alt="Paved greenway trail beside Irwin Creek in Charlotte"}
::

There is nothing better to do on the weekend than enjoy some unspoiled nature, which is what you’ll find on the Buzzard Rock Trail in Latta Nature Preserve, on the shore of Mountain Island Lake. The trail is short and well shaded, with lovely views over the water. Watch out for poison ivy off the trail; otherwise, it’s an excellent choice for everyone in the family! For reviews and directions, visit Tripadvisor:

[https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g49232-d6854482-Reviews-Buzzard_Rock_Trail-Huntersville_North_Carolina.html](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g49232-d6854482-Reviews-Buzzard_Rock_Trail-Huntersville_North_Carolina.html)

## Blythe Landing on Lake Norman
::post-photo{src="/images/photos/lake-norman-roped-walkway.jpg" alt="Roped walkway leading to the water on Lake Norman"}
::

If you want to take out your boat or canoe or just spend some time by Lake Norman, check out Blythe Landing! This Mecklenburg County park has a boat launch, a fishing pier, sand volleyball courts and more. Swimming is not allowed, but you can relax by the water and enjoy some summertime fun with the entire family. Mecklenburg County Park and Recreation lists the park’s directions and hours.

## Birkdale Village
::post-photo{src="/images/photos/huntersville-latta-plantation.jpg" alt="Historic house at Latta Plantation in Huntersville"}
::

For anyone who wants to enjoy a bit of exercise and the outdoors but prefers to stay close to town, visit Birkdale Village. Strolling this outdoor shopping village of shops, restaurants and spas costs nothing, and it often hosts live music and other events. For directions and store listings, visit:

[https://birkdalevillage.com/](https://birkdalevillage.com/)
