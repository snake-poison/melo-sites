Hiring a city of Charlotte claims adjuster is a good way to make sure your insurance company pays everything your policy owes after a property loss. A public adjuster works for the public, meaning policyholders, rather than the insurance company, investigating the loss and negotiating with the insurer on your behalf.

As with hiring any professional, it’s vital that you research your city of Charlotte claims adjuster options carefully, including their license and experience. Hiring the wrong adjuster can cost you, while a professional you can trust, who works diligently for you, can make a real difference to your settlement! Check out some simple but vital tips for finding the right city of Charlotte claims adjuster for your claim.

## When to Hire a City of Charlotte Claims Adjuster
First, note when you want to hire a city of Charlotte claims adjuster, as not every loss needs one. Generally, consider hiring a Charlotte public adjuster if you feel your insurance company is treating you unfairly, if you don’t understand your policy or why the insurer says certain losses aren’t covered, or if your loss is large and complicated.

One reason is that a public adjuster is usually paid a percentage of what the insurer pays on your claim. For smaller claims, that fee can eat up most of whatever extra the adjuster recovers.

For example, suppose a summer thunderstorm damages a few shingles and the insurer’s offer comes in just under your roofer’s estimate. An adjuster might close that gap, but after their fee you would be left with little more than the original offer, and the time it took might not be worth it.

On the other hand, if a hurricane remnant like Helene drops a tree through your Matthews home, or an ice storm bursts the pipes in your Concord business, and the insurer is offering perhaps half of what the repairs will cost, it’s probably time to hire a city of Charlotte claims adjuster! He or she can review the policy and your losses and then negotiate for a higher payout, making their fee a worthwhile investment.

## Check the License of a City of Charlotte Claims Adjuster
Public adjusters are licensed by the state, not the city or county: North Carolina licenses them through its Department of Insurance. Before you sign with any city of Charlotte claims adjuster, ask for their license number and check it with the Department of Insurance to make sure it is current.

It’s also good to check the Better Business Bureau and online reviews, including Google, for complaints about your prospective adjuster. Sites like LinkedIn let people leave recommendations for professionals, too. A current license is the minimum; reviews and references tell you how the adjuster actually treats clients.

## Choose a Charlotte NC Public Claims Adjuster With Relevant Experience
When shopping around for a Charlotte NC public claims adjuster, choose one with experience in your type of claim and, ideally, with your insurance company. While most public adjusters will work diligently on any claim, one who has handled many house fires, for instance, will know where smoke damage hides and how fire claims are usually valued.

A city of Charlotte claims adjuster should also expect prospective clients to ask for references! He or she should be able to put you in touch with past clients who had similar losses.

When you check references, ask how those clients got in touch with the adjuster. After a big storm, out-of-town adjusters and contractors often go door to door through neighborhoods from Gastonia to Monroe, pressing homeowners to sign on the spot. Some may not be licensed in North Carolina at all.

A qualified adjuster will give you time to read the contract, answer your questions and check their license before you sign. Be wary of anyone who won’t.

## Choose a Charlotte Public Adjuster You Feel Comfortable With!
Most policyholders hire a Charlotte public adjuster to negotiate with their insurer for a fairer settlement than the one offered. Another advantage of working with a public adjuster is that they know the “ins and outs” of insurance policies and can make sense of the fine print, as well as the details of your loss.

In turn, a city of Charlotte claims adjuster should be able to explain your policy in simple, easy-to-understand terms. While they’re not lawyers, they can tell you what to expect throughout the process and which mistakes to avoid, such as throwing out damaged belongings before they’ve been photographed and listed. He or she can also explain why the insurer’s offer might differ from what you expected.

This guidance matters most for anyone who doesn’t fully understand their policy or how a claim is negotiated, which is why it’s important to choose a city of Charlotte claims adjuster you feel comfortable with! If a potential adjuster rushes you through appointments or doesn’t answer questions clearly, move on to another candidate.

Keep all these tips in mind when shopping for a [Charlotte claims adjuster](/claims-adjuster/). Find one with a current North Carolina license, relevant experience and good references, and who makes you feel comfortable throughout the process.
