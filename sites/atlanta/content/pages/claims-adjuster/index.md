## Builders Risk Policy & Claims Adjusters in Atlanta

You may have heard of builder's risk insurance by the name "course of construction." It's a type of property insurance that is added to your existing policy when there is new construction going on at your home or commercial location.

The coverage you can attach to your insurance policy will protect your investment against unforeseen damages to the building itself, the building materials that are waiting to be put up that are on-site, or even the construction materials that you've purchased and are in transport. Under most policies, you will be able to recover damages as a result of:

- Vandalism
- Theft
- Hail
- Lightning
- Wind
- Fire
- Explosions
- Aircraft or vehicles
