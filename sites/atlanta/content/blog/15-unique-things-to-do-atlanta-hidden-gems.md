Are you looking for unique things to do in Atlanta? Home to CNN, Coca-Cola, and many national businesses, Atlanta boasts a strong economy while also offering much to see and do! Whether you’re planning a trip to the area or are a lifelong resident, check out these 15 unique things to do in Atlanta, and be sure you don’t overlook any hidden gems the city has to offer.

## 1. Skyview Atlanta

If you love Ferris wheels but get a bit nervous sitting in their open cars, check out the private, enclosed gondolas on Skyview Atlanta. These gondolas offer a feeling of safety when riding the wheel, without sacrificing a view of the amazing city below.

## 2. Fernbank Science Center

More than just your average museum, Fernbank Science Center boasts a 70-foot planetarium dome, the largest in the Southeast region. The dome offers an up-close look at outer space, making you feel as if you’re flying through an asteroid belt or getting up close with a black hole!

## 3. Bury the Hatchet

::post-photo{src="/wp-content/uploads/2021/01/atlanta-things-to-do-45-1.jpg" alt="bury the hatchet things to do atlanta"}
::

If you’re looking for a way to de-stress while enjoying some old-fashioned physical exertion, check out Bury the Hatchet. Axe throwing experts show you the ropes and then you get to throw your own axes at nearby targets, or enjoy some group exercises and competitions!

## 4. The Sipping Plant

For those looking to do something with their hands, check out the Sipping Plant, where you can sign up to make your own terrarium. These small, glass-enclosed gardens are an excellent way of bringing some greens inside your home, and you’ll feel especially proud to show them off after making one yourself!

## 5. Antique Biplane Rides

::post-photo{src="/wp-content/uploads/2021/01/atlanta-things-to-do-46-1.jpg" alt="flying a biplane in atlanta georgia"}
::

For a bit of nostalgia, check out Biplane Rides Over Atlanta. As the name says, you can enjoy a flight over the city in a fully restored biplane, offering a unique experience in the sky!

## 6. The Michael C. Carlos Museum

For those who love ancient art, be sure to visit the Michael C. Carlos Museum. There you can check out artifacts from ancient Rome, Egypt, Greece, and surrounding areas, including a marble portrait of Tiberius, the second Roman Emperor.

## 7. Escape Woods

::post-photo{src="/wp-content/uploads/2021/01/atlanta-things-to-do-47-1.jpg" alt="escape room in atlanta"}
::

Escape games are a favorite attraction for many, and Escape Woods lets you enjoy this thrill outdoors! At Escape Woods, you can race against the clock to find a buried artifact or work to escape an abandoned bunker. All games are fully supervised, so get your friends together and try your hand at a bit of competitive, outdoor fun.

## 8. Atlanta Glass Tree House

For those who love tree houses and need a place to stay for the night, check out the Atlanta Glass Tree House. This house is available for overnight rentals and allows you to enjoy a memorable night high among the treetops!

## 9. 54 Columns

The 54 Columns art installation consists of tall columns set in the historic Old Fourth Ward. The piece has been the subject of much speculation since its installation, with some wondering its meaning and significance. If you’re in the city, why not check it out and see what it means to you?

## 10. The Atlanta White House

::post-photo{src="/wp-content/uploads/2021/01/atlanta-things-to-do-48-1.jpg" alt="atlanta white house"}
::

If you’ve always wanted to visit the nation’s capital but don’t have the time or funds, check out The Atlanta White House! This attraction is a 3/4 scale replica of the White House in Washington D.C., and it can give you a glimpse of what the true White House looks like both inside and out.

## 11. The CDC Museum

This little gem is not for the faint of heart, but if you love sci-fi or science itself, be sure to visit the CDC Museum. This museum houses artifacts dedicated to the Centers for Disease Control, showcasing its work over the years in controlling or eradicating smallpox, measles, and much more.

## 12. Center for Puppetry Arts

The largest museum dedicated to puppets in the world, the Center for Puppetry Arts in Atlanta also houses a showcase dedicated to Jim Henson, creator of The Muppets.

## 13. The Cator Woolford Gardens

::post-photo{src="/wp-content/uploads/2021/01/atlanta-things-to-do-49-1.jpg" alt="cator woolford gardens atlanta ga"}
::

A true hidden gem, this 32-acre park offers a tranquil setting for visitors. You’ll find any number of native flowers in the garden as well as plenty of soft grass, perfect for a picnic or just relaxing under the sun.

## 14. Robert C. Williams Paper Museum

The Robert C. Williams Paper Museum traces the medium’s origins all the way back to Sumerians and Egyptians. The museum houses some 100,000 artifacts related to paper, so if you’re an avid writer or artist, it’s a must-see!

## 15. The Switchyards Ping Pong Club

::post-photo{src="/wp-content/uploads/2021/01/atlanta-things-to-do-50-1.jpg" alt="ping pong playing in georgia"}
::

Located a few steps down at the corner of Williams and Ted Turner Street, this club showcases its interior setting with wide windows leading off to the street. Home to local tournaments and private play, if you love ping pong you must visit The Switchyards Ping Pong Club.
